title: What is Sling
description: Understand how Sling connects rostering, wage estimates, and payroll.
---

Sling is the workforce management platform used to roster, communicate with, and coordinate casual employees. Its published shift data also feeds the Fizz Kidz payroll export.

## One account, many locations

- All studios operate inside a single Fizz Kidz Sling account so rosters, payroll integrations, and reporting stay centralised.
- Staff are granted access only to their home location. They cannot view or edit shifts outside that site unless they are explicitly given multi-location access.
- **Staff** can view their roster, acknowledge shifts, and message the team.
- **Managers** can publish rosters, review wage estimates, approve time off, and coordinate messaging for their location.

## Everyday use

- Sling is our only rostering tool for casual employees; every shift must be created and published here.
- Internal announcements, DMs, and group chats all run through Sling’s messaging so we keep conversations tied to the roster context.
- The Portal turns published shifts into a reviewed CSV. It does not send shifts directly to Xero. See [Payroll](/people/payroll).
- Shift labels control Xero pay items and tracking. See [Sling shifts and payroll](/tools/sling/sling-payroll).
- Budget estimates use wage data synced from Xero each Friday. See [Sling budgeting](/tools/sling/sling-budgeting).
- Staff request time away through Sling’s **Time Off** feature. The **Unavailability** module is disabled because it does not support our process. Time off requests must be submitted at least 14 days in advance and approved by a manager; approved entries help roster planners avoid clashes.

## What is automatic

- Xero ordinary rates sync into Sling each Friday for wage estimates.
- Active casual employees are assigned wage estimates for every supported shift position.
- The payroll generator reads published shifts for the selected period and business.

## What is manual

- Employee accounts and invitations. The [onboarding workflow](/people/onboarding) does not create Sling users.
- Correct locations, roles, shift positions, times, publication, and notes.
- Reviewing the payroll CSV and importing it to Xero.

## Guardrails

- Do **not** add, edit, or delete shift types or locations. Finance maintains these to keep the payroll integration stable.
- Keep roster edits inside your assigned location unless you have explicit approval to support another site.
- Ensure each casual employee's Sling email matches Xero so payroll can identify them.
- Review the published roster before payroll cut-off so the export uses the correct shifts and pay codes.
