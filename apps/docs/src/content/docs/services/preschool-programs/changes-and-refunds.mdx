## How the refund is calculated

For cancellations at least 48 hours before the session, the refund is the amount paid minus the newly calculated value
of all sessions still booked. Whole-term eligibility and the original discount code are included in that calculation.

- A whole-term cancellation reprices the remaining discounted dates at the standard session price.
- A refund can therefore be less than one session's displayed price, or $0.
- Split payments are refunded to the original gift card and card, up to the amount paid with each.
- The cancellation email states the refunded amount when a refund applies.

<Aside type="note" title="Why a term cancellation can return $0">
  Imagine the family paid a discounted whole-term total. After one discounted date is removed, the remaining dates
  return to standard price. If those remaining dates are now worth as much as, or more than, the amount already paid,
  there is no difference to refund yet.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip" title="Whole-term refund amount">
  The refund is based on the value of everything still booked, not simply the cancelled session's displayed price.
</Aside>
