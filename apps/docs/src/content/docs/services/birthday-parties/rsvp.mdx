The invitation link has two views: guests see the public invitation and RSVP form; the signed-in host and signed-in Fizz
Kidz staff see the live guest list.

<Aside type="caution" title="Fizz Kidz does not monitor allergy arrangements">
  Allergy information is shown to the party host for planning. Guests are told that the host must arrange allergy needs
  directly. Staff can view the information, but the RSVP system is not an automatic allergy alert or approval process.
</Aside>

## Create and share an invitation

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>Open the invitation link</h3>
      <p>
        The booking confirmation contains a durable host link. If an invitation already exists, it opens that
        invitation; otherwise it starts the design flow.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Choose the details and design</h3>
      <p>
        Party details are prefilled from the booking. The host can set the RSVP contact, deadline and whether to receive
        an email for each guest response.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Sign in and finish</h3>
      <p>
        Saving requires a Fizz Kidz Portal account sign-in. The account becomes the invitation owner. On the first save,
        the system links the invitation to the booking and emails the booking's parent address with the share link.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Share the stable link</h3>
      <p>
        The host can copy the link, share it by WhatsApp, SMS or email, or download a PNG to print. The downloaded
        invite includes a QR code to the same public page.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Design changes keep the guest list">
  Editing the invitation or choosing another design keeps the same invitation ID. Existing links and printed QR codes
  continue to work, and the RSVPs remain attached to the booking.
</Aside>

## What guests submit

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="One reply, multiple children" icon="information">
    <p>A family can include more than one child and choose attending or not attending for each child.</p>
  </Card>
  <Card title="Required contact details" icon="information">
    <p>Guest submissions require the parent's name, email and mobile, plus each child's name and date of birth.</p>
  </Card>
  <Card title="Allergies and message" icon="warning">
    <p>Attending children require a yes/no allergy answer. Allergy details and a message can be included.</p>
  </Card>
  <Card title="Marketing preference" icon="email">
    <p>
      The form includes a marketing checkbox. The response is passed to Zoho as the contact's opt-in or opt-out
      preference; do not promise subscription to a particular marketing campaign.
    </p>
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## What happens after a guest RSVP

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>The RSVP is saved</h3>
      <p>
        The response is stored under the party booking. Guest-list totals count children, not form submissions, so a
        single family can add several attending or not-attending responses.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Zoho CRM is updated</h3>
      <p>
        The system creates or updates a Birthday Party Guest parent and child record for every child, including the
        branch and marketing preference.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Confirmation emails are sent</h3>
      <p>
        The guest receives a summary with each child's response, allergy notes, date, time, venue and host contact. If
        host notifications are enabled, the booking's parent email also receives the response details.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

## Host and staff controls

| Action                                               | Host account | Signed-in Fizz Kidz staff          |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| View totals, contact details, messages and allergies | Yes          | Yes                                |
| Change attending/not attending on an existing entry  | Yes          | Yes                                |
| Delete a child or response                           | Yes          | Yes                                |
| Add an RSVP on behalf of a guest                     | Yes          | No host-only button                |
| Turn response emails on or off                       | Yes          | No host-only switch                |
| Preview and share the invitation                     | Yes          | Available from the management view |

Host-entered RSVPs deliberately skip date of birth, guest confirmation email and Zoho guest creation. Use this when the
host receives a reply by phone or message.

<Aside type="caution" title="Edits are silent">
  Changing a response or deleting it from the guest list updates the saved list only. It does not email the guest and
  does not reverse the guest records already written to Zoho. Tell the family directly when confirmation matters.
</Aside>

## Common problems

### A guest needs to change a response

Guests cannot reopen and edit a submitted RSVP. The host or a signed-in team member can change the status or delete the
entry from the management view. If the family needs to supply new contact or allergy details, the host should confirm
those directly.

### The host sees the guest form

They are not signed into the invitation owner's account. Ask them to sign in using the same account used to finalise the
invitation, then reopen the invitation link.

### A team member cannot see the guest list

Confirm they are signed into the Portal and have a Fizz Kidz organisation role. Opening the link while signed out shows
the public guest experience.

### The invitation will not load

Open the booking in the Portal and use <strong>Get invitation link</strong> rather than reusing a copied or shortened
link. If the booking has been deleted, its invitation image, invitation record and RSVPs are also deleted.

### A downloaded invite is old

Preview the current invitation and download it again. The public link and QR code remain stable, but an old PNG does not
change after a redesign.
