Party bookings in the Portal feed Google Calendar, customer emails, Zoho CRM, invitations and the Party Form. Make the
change in the Portal rather than editing an automatic calendar event by hand.

## Find a booking

1. Open **Portal > Bookings**.
2. Choose the party date and, if needed, the location filter.
3. Expand the booking under the correct studio.
4. A direct booking link containing `?id=` opens that specific record and moves the page to its date and location.

## Create a booking

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>Enter the family and child details</h3>
      <p>
        Add every birthday child separately. Check each birthday and the age they will be turning; those records also
        feed the Calendar title, confirmation wording and Zoho CRM.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Enter date, time and party setup</h3>
      <p>
        Select studio or mobile, the Fizz location, party length and mobile address where applicable. Studio bookings
        also require the included-food or self-catered choice.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Choose whether to send the confirmation</h3>
      <p>
        <strong>Send confirmation email</strong> is selected by default. Clear it only when there is a deliberate reason
        not to contact the customer yet.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Save the booking</h3>
      <p>
        The Portal saves the booking, creates its Google Calendar event, and creates or updates the confirmed Birthday
        Party deal, parent and children in Zoho CRM.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Calendar" icon="open-book">
    <p>
      The event title contains the parent, birthday child display and mobile. It links back to the Portal booking and
      uses the studio address or mobile-party address.
    </p>
  </Card>
  <Card title="Zoho CRM" icon="rocket">
    <p>
      An existing enquiry deal can be confirmed using its Zoho deal ID. Without one, the system creates or upserts a
      confirmed booking deal and writes the new deal ID back to the booking.
    </p>
  </Card>
  <Card title="Confirmation email" icon="email">
    <p>
      The email includes booking details, lead-up contact, cancellation terms, invitations and eligible order links. It
      comes from the booking flow only when the checkbox is selected.
    </p>
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Change a booking

1. Expand the booking and select **Edit**.
2. Change the available fields. The location is locked in the existing-booking form.
3. Select **Save**.

### What changes automatically

| Change | Google Calendar | Customer email | Zoho CRM |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Date or start time | Event start/end updates | Updated-time confirmation is sent | Party date updates when the booking has a Zoho deal ID |
| Party length | Event end updates | Updated-time confirmation is sent | No length update in this workflow |
| Mobile address | Event location updates | No automatic email | No address update in this workflow |
| Parent contact, child, food, notes, creations or add-ons | Event title may use changed parent/child/mobile values when saved | No automatic email | No general contact/deal sync in this workflow |

## Delete a booking

<Aside type="danger" title="Delete is not a customer cancellation workflow">
  Deleting removes system records. It does not send the family a cancellation email, charge a cancellation fee or issue
  a refund. Handle those customer and payment steps separately.
</Aside>

Deleting the booking:

- removes the Google Calendar event;
- marks a linked Zoho party deal as **Lost Booking** with the selected reason;
- removes the invitation record and stored image when one exists;
- removes every RSVP under the booking; and
- deletes the Portal booking.

Choose the lost reason carefully because it is recorded in Zoho.
