The after-school program journey joins three systems. The Portal holds the enrolment and family details, Acuity holds
the individual class appointments, and Square handles the invoice later.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Portal" icon="open-book">
    One enrolment record is created for each child. It holds their program, family details, continuation choice, invoice
    link, pickup people, and pickup records.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Acuity" icon="calendar">
    Acuity supplies the programs, dates, prices, and capacity. A successful enrolment creates an appointment for every
    remaining published session in the selected term.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Square" icon="approve-check-circle">
    Square is not involved when the family first enrols. If they continue after the free trial, staff send a Square
    invoice for the chosen number of weeks.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Journey at a glance

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>The family chooses a program</h3>
      <p>
        The enrolment form reads the currently bookable school or studio programs from Acuity. It also uses Acuity's
        upcoming classes to show the term length, total price, and whether the class has space.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The form chooses enrolment or waitlist</h3>
      <p>
        If the class has space, the family completes the full enrolment form. If it is full, they see a shorter waitlist
        form instead. See <a href="/services/after-school-programs/enrolments-and-waitlists/">Enrolments and waitlists</a>.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The enrolment is created without a card</h3>
      <p>
        No card details are collected. The child's remaining term sessions are booked, and the family receives an
        enrolment confirmation and parent portal link.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The family tries the program</h3>
      <p>
        The free trial is part of the term appointments already created in Acuity. The family is not invoiced during the
        initial enrolment step.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Staff ask whether they are continuing</h3>
      <p>
        Staff send a continuation email from the invoicing view. The family's choice is written back to the Portal. A
        yes response prepares the enrolment for invoicing; a no response alerts the bookings team for follow-up. See
        <a href="/services/after-school-programs/after-the-trial/">After the trial</a>.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Square handles payment</h3>
      <p>
        Staff select the number of weeks and send the invoice. Square emails the family and the Portal reads Square's
        latest status. See <a href="/services/after-school-programs/invoicing/">Invoicing</a>.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Attendance continues week by week</h3>
      <p>
        Parents can mark an absence and maintain pickup people in their portal. Staff check children in and out against
        the matching Acuity session. See
        <a href="/services/after-school-programs/parent-portal-and-attendance/">Parent portal and attendance</a>.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

<Aside type="note" title="One child, one linked journey">
  When a family enrols more than one child, each child gets a separate Portal enrolment, set of Acuity appointments,
  parent portal link, continuation choice, and invoice.
</Aside>
