The continuation step is started by staff. From the after-school invoicing view, staff select the relevant enrolments
and send an email asking each family whether their child will continue for the term.

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>
        Staff send the continuation email <Badge text="Manual start" variant="note" size="small" />
      </h3>
      <p>
        The email includes the child, program, current term total, and separate links for continuing or not continuing.
        The Portal shows that the continuation email has been sent.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The family chooses yes or no</h3>
      <p>
        Opening either link updates the child's continuation choice on the Portal enrolment. The invoicing list then
        shows <strong>YES</strong>, <strong>NO</strong>, or <strong>UNKNOWN</strong>.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Staff complete the matching action</h3>
      <p>
        A yes response is ready for a Square invoice. A no response sends a notification to the bookings team and must
        still be followed by the Portal unenrolment action.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="YES: continuing" icon="approve-check-circle">
    The family sees confirmation that their term place is confirmed and that an invoice will follow. Their choice alone
    does not send the invoice; staff do that from the invoicing view.
  </Card>
  <Card title="NO: not continuing" icon="information">
    The Portal records the choice and alerts the bookings team. Check-in screens show the child as not continuing, but
    the Acuity appointments still exist until staff use the full unenrolment action.
  </Card>
  <Card title="UNKNOWN: no choice yet" icon="warning">
    The family has not selected yes or no. Staff decide whether to send another continuation email or contact them
    manually.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Aside type="caution" title="A no response is not the final cancellation">
  Recording <strong>not continuing</strong> does not cancel the child's Acuity appointments, void an unpaid invoice, or
  make the Portal enrolment inactive. Use the Portal's <strong>Unenrol</strong> action to complete those changes.
</Aside>

## Completing an unenrolment

The Portal unenrolment action:

- Cancels every linked after-school appointment in Acuity.
- Cancels the linked Square invoice when it is still cancellable.
- Marks the Portal enrolment inactive so it leaves active check-in and invoicing lists.
- Optionally sends the family an unenrolment confirmation email.

Paid invoices are not refunded by this action. If a refund is appropriate, it must be handled separately in Square and
will continue to appear as paid in the Portal. See [Invoicing](/services/after-school-programs/invoicing/) for invoice
statuses and refund implications.

For the earlier steps, see [Enrolments and waitlists](/services/after-school-programs/enrolments-and-waitlists/).
