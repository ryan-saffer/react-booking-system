title: Parent portal and attendance
description: How parent updates and staff check-in share the same enrolment and Acuity sessions.

import { Aside, Card, CardGrid, Steps } from '@astrojs/starlight/components'

Each child has a parent portal linked to their Portal enrolment. The link is emailed when the enrolment is created, and
staff can also open it from the child's expanded details in the invoicing view.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Enrolment and invoice" icon="open-book">
    The portal shows the program, child, expected term price, and invoice status. An unpaid invoice includes a payment
    link; a paid invoice can still be viewed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Weekly attendance" icon="calendar">
    The parent can turn attendance off for an individual session. This applies a not-attending marker to the matching
    Acuity appointment so it also appears in staff check-in.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pickup people" icon="people">
    The parent can add or remove approved pickup people. The updated list is saved directly to the enrolment and appears
    to staff during pickup.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Aside type="note" title="An absence is not a cancellation">
  Turning off one week only marks that Acuity appointment as not attending. It does not remove the appointment, change
  the invoice, or create a credit or refund.
</Aside>

## Staff attendance flow

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>Select the program and class date</h3>
      <p>
        The check-in view reads the class sessions and appointments from Acuity, then matches them to active Portal
        enrolments.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Review the child's current details</h3>
      <p>
        Staff can see absences, continuation choices, allergies, anaphylaxis information, photography permission,
        support notes, emergency contact details, and pickup people.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Sign the child in</h3>
      <p>
        Signing in changes the Acuity appointment's attendance marker. Children marked not attending are clearly shown
        and do not receive the normal sign-in action.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Record pickup</h3>
      <p>
        For after-school sessions, staff select the collecting person and save their signature. The enrolment keeps the
        pickup person, signature, and time against that specific appointment, while Acuity is marked signed out.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

The sign-out choices include the enrolled parent, the extra pickup people maintained in the parent portal, and a staff
option. The enrolled parent is included automatically. Sessions beginning before 2 pm are treated as lunchtime classes
and are signed out without a pickup signature.

For full-term removal rather than a single absence, follow [After the trial](/services/after-school-programs/after-the-trial/).
For payment questions, see [Invoicing](/services/after-school-programs/invoicing/).
