After-school invoicing begins after the free trial. The Portal selects the enrolment and calculates the amount; Square
creates, emails, and tracks the actual invoice.

## Sending an invoice

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>Choose the program and enrolments</h3>
      <p>
        The invoicing view shows active Portal enrolments for the selected program, together with their continuation and
        current Square invoice status.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Select the number of weeks</h3>
      <p>
        Staff choose the number of weeks to charge. The Portal multiplies that number by the per-week price saved when
        the child enrolled. This allows a mid-term enrolment to be billed for fewer weeks.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Square publishes and emails the invoice</h3>
      <p>
        Square creates the customer and invoice, accepts card payment, and emails the payment link. The invoice is due
        three days after it is sent and includes three scheduled reminders.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The Portal stores the Square invoice link</h3>
      <p>
        The new invoice ID is saved against the enrolment, the continuation choice is set to yes, and both staff and the
        parent portal can read the latest status from Square.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Choose the weeks before sending">
  The number of weeks is chosen by staff; it is not automatically taken from the full appointment count. Sending again
  creates a new invoice and cancels the previous invoice when it is still cancellable. A paid invoice remains paid, and
  a new invoice can still be created, so review the current invoice before sending another.
</Aside>

<Aside type="note" title="Reminders are automatic">
  Square sends the three reminders using the schedule included with the invoice. Staff do not need to send them
  separately.
</Aside>

## Portal statuses

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="NOT SENT" icon="information">
    No Square invoice ID is stored on the enrolment. The parent portal shows the expected full-term price but no payment
    button.
  </Card>
  <Card title="UNPAID" icon="warning">
    A Square invoice exists but is not currently reported as paid or cancelled. Staff can open the invoice in Square;
    the parent can use the payment link.
  </Card>
  <Card title="PAID" icon="approve-check-circle">
    The invoice is paid. Partially refunded invoices also appear as <strong>PAID</strong> in the Portal.
  </Card>
  <Card title="VOID" icon="seti:lock">
    Square reports the invoice as cancelled. The Portal keeps the link so staff can inspect the invoice history.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Unenrolments and refunds

<Badge text="Acuity" variant="note" size="small" />{' '}
<Badge text="Portal" variant="tip" size="small" />{' '}
<Badge text="Square" variant="caution" size="small" />

Using the Portal's full unenrolment action cancels the child's appointments, marks the enrolment inactive, and cancels a
linked invoice when it is still cancellable.

It does **not** refund a paid invoice. Full and partial refunds are separate manual actions in Square, and a partially
refunded invoice continues to appear as <strong>PAID</strong> in the Portal.

<Aside type="note" title="Do not use an absence to adjust an invoice">
  A week marked as not attending remains part of the enrolment and does not create a credit or refund. Attendance and
  billing are separate. See
  <a href="/services/after-school-programs/parent-portal-and-attendance/">Parent portal and attendance</a>.
</Aside>

See [After the trial](/services/after-school-programs/after-the-trial/) for the continuation step that comes before
invoicing.
