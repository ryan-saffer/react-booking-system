Incursion preparation forms are sent automatically. Each day at 8:30 am, upcoming incursions within the next 22 days
become eligible for the form email.

<Aside type="tip" title="Think of 22 days as a sending window">
  The form is not sent at an exact minute 22 days before the incursion. It is sent during the first daily run after the
  booking enters the 22-day window.
</Aside>

## The automated journey

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>
        Eligible incursions are selected <Badge text="Daily 8:30 am" variant="note" size="small" />
      </h3>
      <p>Only future incursions receive this form. Standard events do not.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Sibling slots are grouped together</h3>
      <p>
        Every slot in the booking is included in one email to the booking contact. The email lists all current session
        dates and times and links to one shared form.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The event is marked as sent</h3>
      <p>The sent marker applies to every sibling slot, so the booking receives one preparation form email.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The customer submits final details</h3>
      <p>
        The form captures the organisation and address again, total children, room or location, parking, expected
        learning, teacher presence, additional information, and how they heard about Fizz Kidz.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The update is shared</h3>
      <p>
        The submission marks the incursion form complete and updates those preparation details across all sibling Portal
        slots. The number of children also becomes the updated students-per-session value.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Both sides receive a summary</h3>
      <p>
        The customer receives a confirmation of their answers and all slots. The responsible Fizz Kidz studio receives
        the booking and preparation summary, with the customer's address available for a direct reply.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Portal" icon="open-book">
    Stores whether the form was sent and completed, plus all submitted preparation answers. The completed answers appear
    in the event's Form Results section.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Google Calendar" icon="calendar">
    Keeps the scheduled slot entries. A preparation form submission does not rewrite those calendar entries.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Zoho" icon="rocket">
    The submission updates the incursion student count on the linked deal.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
